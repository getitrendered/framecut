## Video transforms, Footage tray, and Text List fonts

- Select a video clip and open **Inspector → Transform** to adjust scale, rotation, width and height stretch, position, and opacity.
- Choose **Fit inside**, **Stretch to screen**, **Fit by width**, or **Fit by height**. These controls work in preview and export, and scale, rotation, position, and opacity also support keyframes in Animation.
- Open **Footage tray** at the bottom of the timeline and drag media from the current project onto the tracks. You can also drop audio onto the timeline to add an audio track.
- Choose a font in **Text List** and preview it before adding the list. The selected font applies to every generated title.
