## Background keying

- Select a timeline clip and open **Background key** in the Inspector. Pick green, blue, or black, or use **Sample background** to pick a color from the video.
- Tune the matte with **Tolerance**, **Edge softness**, and **Spill suppression**. **Show matte** displays transparent areas in black and opaque areas in white.
- Place a clip on a lower video track to replace the removed screen. To export with transparency, choose **WebM** and enable **Keep transparent background**.
