## Place and align text in the Preview canvas

- Choose **T** and click the Preview canvas to add a transparent title layer at the playhead. Click a title in the canvas to select it and drag it to move it.
- Select multiple titles to move them together or change their position, font, alignment, colors, and other styling in the Inspector. Use **Position in preview** to set a point on the canvas for the selected titles.
- Open **Align** next to **T** for canvas alignment buttons and adjustable horizontal and vertical margin guides. The guides are visible only in the editor.
- Typewriter reveals text in smaller, evenly timed steps and completes the reveal during short clips.
