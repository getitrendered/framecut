## Timeline locks, track mixer, and playhead scrolling

- Lock the whole timeline to protect it from edits, or lock individual tracks using their headers.
- Mute video or audio track sound and hide text tracks from the track headers.
- Use the Sound Design mixer to set video, audio, and master levels for playback and export.
- Select a compatible track before pressing Ctrl+V to paste there at the playhead. Pasting moves to the next open space if a clip is in the way.
- Drag the playhead over the track headers or past the right edge to scroll the timeline automatically.
- Find Closest Loop places the resulting cut on a video layer above the source and keeps the original in place.
