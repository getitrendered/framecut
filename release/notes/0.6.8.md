## Linked compositions

- Use **Add to Main** or drag a composition from the drawer. It appears as one editable clip and previews directly from its source timeline. Trim, move, or copy the group on Main, or choose **Open composition** to edit its contents.
- Choose **Delete** beside a composition to remove it and its linked clips. Any LED walls using it keep their layout and clear that video assignment.
- Open **Background tasks** and choose **Stop** to cancel a composition render or export.
