## Installed fonts and text materials

- Text List and the title Inspector now list installed Windows TrueType and OpenType fonts, including per-user fonts and fonts in collections.
- Text List now includes solid face colors, customizable three-color gradients, and 18 gradient and metal presets. Preview looks such as Gold, Chrome, Copper, and Rose gold before applying them to the whole list.
- Set extrusion depth, direction, and side color. Generated titles keep their appearance in preview and export, and you can edit each title in the Inspector.
