## Text List

- Paste or type one title per line in **Text & Subtitles → Text List**.
- Choose how the titles animate in and out, and set separate durations for the entrance, hold, and exit. Preview the first two titles before adding the list.
- Add up to 500 consecutive, individually editable titles on a title layer in one action. Choose a new layer to place the list at the playhead when an existing layer is occupied.
- Large title sequences render in sections and export as one video.
