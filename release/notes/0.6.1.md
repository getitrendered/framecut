## Save project on demand

- Use **Save** or **Ctrl+S** in any project workspace to save your progress and see a confirmation. Autosave also continues as you work.
- If you are updating directly from 0.5.8, this release also includes the Projects home, custom canvas sizes, compositions, In/Out export ranges, LED Wall simulation, timeline multi-select and repeat copies, unified Import, and Footage removal and restore.
