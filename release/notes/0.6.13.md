## Editable Text Lists and shared title timing

- New Text Lists appear as a single timeline block. Double-click a block to change or reorder lines and edit its styling, entrance, hold duration, and exit. Use **Show individual titles** to work on the clips separately.
- Select multiple titles and open **Animation & timing** in the Inspector to change the timing or animations together.
- Select consecutive titles on one title layer and choose **Group as Text List** to reopen them as one editable list. Grouping keeps their existing text and timing.
