## Import from YouTube

- Open a project and choose **YouTube** next to **Import**. Paste a video or Shorts link, check the available qualities, and choose **Download to project**.
- Framecut joins the video and audio automatically when they come as separate streams. The download appears in the open project’s **Footage**.
- Watch progress in the import dialog or **Background tasks**, and use **Stop** to cancel. Framecut downloads and verifies the helper on first use.
