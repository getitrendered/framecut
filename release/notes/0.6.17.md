## PNG transparency and direct preview editing

- Transparent PNG imports keep their alpha channel in the editor and exports. Reimport PNGs added before this update to recover their transparency from the original files.
- Drag a still image’s timeline edges to adjust how long it stays on screen.
- Click a visible image, video, or title in Preview to select it. Drag to move it, use a corner to scale, or pull the round handle to rotate. Use the side handles to stretch images and videos.
- Double-click a title in Preview to edit it. Click away or press **Ctrl+Enter** to save the edit, or press **Esc** to cancel.
