## Timeline clipboard shortcuts

- **Ctrl+C** copies the selected clip. **Ctrl+X** cuts it and leaves a gap.
- **Ctrl+V** pastes at the playhead on the original track, or at the next open position if the space is occupied.
- Video, title, caption, and audio clips keep their trim and settings. Cut and paste can be undone.
- The same commands are available from the timeline's right-click menu. Text fields continue to use your regular clipboard.
- In Footage, copy a selected asset and paste it into Edit, or cut and paste it between folders.
- In Animation, copy or cut a focused keyframe and paste its value at the playhead in the selected clip.
