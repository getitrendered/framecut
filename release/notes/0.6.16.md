## YouTube audio and video downloads

- Choose **Video + audio**, **Video only**, or **MP3 only** after checking a YouTube link.
- Pick the video resolution or choose an MP3 bitrate from 64 through 320 kbps. MP3 downloads appear as audio clips in the current project’s **Footage** and can be added to an audio track.
- Follow download and conversion progress in the import dialog or **Background tasks**.
